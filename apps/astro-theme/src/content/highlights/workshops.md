The Workshop Den runs all day, both days: small-group sessions where you
build something real and take it home. This year — nest architecture, pattern
recognition in noisy environments, and pacing the long pull.

Have something to teach? The stage is open to every species — first-time
speakers get a mentor from the program committee.
