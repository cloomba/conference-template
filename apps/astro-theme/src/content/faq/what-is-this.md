---
question: Is Fauna Forum a real event?
order: 5
---

No — Fauna Forum is a fictional conference. This whole site is the live demo
of the open-source **Cloomba conference template**: everything you see (the
agenda, speakers, tickets, coupons, registration) is powered by the
[Cloomba](https://cloomba.com) event platform, exactly as it would be for your
real conference. Feel free to register — no payment is ever taken.
