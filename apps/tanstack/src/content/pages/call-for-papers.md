The stage is open to **every species and every experience level** — first-time
speakers get a dedicated mentor from the program committee (ask for Oleg; he
has arms to spare).

## What we're looking for

- **Leadership & pack dynamics** — leading without barking orders
- **Engineering habitats** — building things that survive the seasons
- **Energy & pacing** — hibernation-friendly roadmaps, sustainable on-call
- **Play & culture** — what keeps a colony worth belonging to

Talks run 45 minutes, workshops 90. If your idea doesn't fit a track, send it
anyway — the best sessions never do.

## How to submit

Email your title, a short abstract (5–10 sentences), and a line about
yourself to [team@fauna-forum.example](mailto:team@fauna-forum.example?subject=Talk%20proposal)
with the subject **"Talk proposal"**. The committee reviews on a rolling
basis; you'll hear back within two weeks.
