## Getting there

The Grand Menagerie sits on the south bank of the Danube, a short walk, trot,
or glide from the city centre.

- **By wing** — the aviary deck on the roof is open from dawn. Larger raptors
  are asked to announce their approach.
- **By land** — trams 1 and 3 stop at the front gate. Paws, hooves, and
  slithering all welcome aboard.
- **By water** — the riverside jetty accommodates everything from otters to
  the occasional confused dolphin.

## Where to stay

Partner burrows, nests, and dens near the venue offer conference rates —
mention **Fauna Forum** when booking. The hibernation-grade suites at Burrow &
Sons sell out early every year.

## Visas & paperwork

Migratory attendees crossing flyway borders should check their ring-tag
paperwork is current. The organizers can provide invitation letters for
customs — request one from the front desk.
