Fauna Forum brings together hundreds of decision-makers from across the
animal kingdom — pack leaders, colony founders, and the engineers who keep
every habitat running. Sponsoring puts your brand at the watering hole where
they all meet.

## Tiers

- **Platinum** — the naming perch: logo on the main stage, a keynote-adjacent
  slot, and the biggest table at the Evening Howl.
- **Gold** — logo across the venue and site, a booth in the main hall, four
  passes.
- **Silver** — logo on the site and in the program, two passes.
- **Bronze & Community** — for young dens and non-profits: site logo and a
  pass, priced so nobody is left out of the ecosystem.

Every tier includes our genuine gratitude and first pick of next year's
dates.

## Talk to us

Write to [team@fauna-forum.example](mailto:team@fauna-forum.example?subject=Sponsoring)
with the subject **"Sponsoring"** and we'll send the full prospectus within a
day or two.
